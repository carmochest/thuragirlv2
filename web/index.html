FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from an accounting firm?

It's the same monthly work, but you see your numbers every day instead of weeks later, and answers take seconds, not days. Anything the law says needs a licensed auditor is done by one, assigned by ThuraGirl.

I already have a company. Can I use it?

Yes. Enter your 13-digit tax ID when you sign up. ThuraGirl pulls your company's records from DBD, and you confirm them.

Which banks work?

Any Thai bank that lets you download a statement as a CSV or Excel file. Download it from your bank's app or website, then upload it to ThuraGirl.

Does anything get filed without me?

No. ThuraGirl prepares every filing and every month-end, then waits for your approval.

Is there a mobile app?

ThuraGirl works in the browser on any phone or computer, with nothing to install. iPhone and Android apps are planned.
