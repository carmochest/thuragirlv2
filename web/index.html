FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from an accounting firm?

The same monthly work, but you see your numbers every day instead of weeks later, and answers take seconds instead of days. Work the law says needs a licensed auditor is done by one, arranged by ThuraGirl.

I already have a company. Can I use it?

Yes. Enter your 13-digit tax ID when you sign up. ThuraGirl pulls your company's records from DBD and you confirm them.

What do I need to register a new company?

Three name choices, details of the founders and shareholders, a registered address and your capital. You fill it in inside the app, and ThuraGirl prepares the documents.

Which banks work?

Any Thai bank that lets you download a statement as a CSV or Excel file. Download it from your bank's app or website and upload it to ThuraGirl.

Does anything get filed without me?

No. ThuraGirl prepares each filing and each month-end, and waits for you to approve it.

Is there a mobile app?

ThuraGirl works in the browser on any phone or computer, in day or night mode. Native iPhone and Android apps are planned.
